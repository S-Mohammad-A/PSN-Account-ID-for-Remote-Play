PSN Account ID

Extract your PlayStation Network Account ID in Base64 format

1

Sign in to PlayStation

Click the button below to open the official Sony login page. Sign in with your PSN account. After signing in you will see a page that says redirect.

Your password is entered only on Sony’s official site and is never sent to this tool.

2

Paste the redirect URL

After a successful login, copy the full address from the browser’s address bar (it usually starts with https://remoteplay.dl.playstation.net/remoteplay/redirect?code=...) and paste it into the box below.

✓

Result

Account ID extracted successfully. You can use it in Chiaki or other Remote Play apps.
